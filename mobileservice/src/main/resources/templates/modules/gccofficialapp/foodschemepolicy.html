GREATER CHENNAI CORPORATION
Privacy Policy

The Greater Chennai Corporation has developed the FMSC (Free Meal Scheme for Conservancy Workers) Application as a free service. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for official use by conservancy workers and authorized personnel.

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information of users who access and use this application.

By using this application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Information Collection and Use

To ensure effective delivery of services under the Free Meal Scheme, we may collect certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to:

  • Identification and authentication of users
  • Ensuring proper distribution and tracking of meals
  • Monitoring and improving service delivery
  • Administrative and reporting purposes

The collected data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill these purposes.

Device Permissions

The application may request the following permissions:

  • Location: To verify the user’s presence at designated locations for availing the scheme .
  • Camera : To capture images for attendance or verification, if applicable .
  • Storage: To store and retrieve relevant application data .

Certain features of the application may not function properly if these permissions are denied.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services for analytics or technical support. These services may collect limited information necessary for improving app performance. Users are encouraged to review the respective privacy policies of such providers.

Log Data

In case of errors or technical issues, the application may collect Log Data such as:

  • Device IP address
  • Device type and operating system
  • App usage details
  • Date and time of access

This data is used only for troubleshooting and improving system performance.

Data Security

We are committed to ensuring the security of your personal information. Appropriate technical and administrative measures are implemented to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, please note that no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or trade user data. Personal information may be shared only:

  • With authorized government departments for official purposes
  • With service providers assisting in application operations (under strict confidentiality)
  • When required by law or legal processes
Cookies

This application does not directly use cookies. However, third-party tools integrated into the app may use cookies for analytics and performance improvement.

Links to External Sites

The application may contain links to external websites. The Greater Chennai Corporation is not responsible for the privacy practices of such external sites. Users are advised to review their privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be posted within the application and will take effect immediately upon posting.
